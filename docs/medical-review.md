# SafeIndia: medical and first-aid review

Thank you for reviewing this. SafeIndia (https://safe-india.vercel.app) is a free website that tells people in India what to do in an emergency. These guides are read by members of the public, often under stress, and can be forwarded on WhatsApp, so a mistake can spread widely.

## What we are asking
For each step below, tick it if it is correct and safe for a member of the public, or write what it should say. Please pay most attention to the questions marked **Question**.

- **Steps 1 to 3 and the first "Do NOT" point of each guide are copied into WhatsApp messages on their own.** They must be safe even without the rest of the guide.
- **Every guide names an official source.** Please tell us if a guide does not actually match that source, or if there is a better official source we should cite.
- If something important is missing, please add it at the end of that guide.

When you have finished, please send this document back, with your name, qualification and the date, so the site can say who reviewed the guides and when.
